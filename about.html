About Us

BUILT BY THE COMMUNITY

SIM Racing NG exists to give Nigerian sim racers a home — to race, to learn, and to be seen.

Our Mission

To make competitive sim racing accessible, welcoming, and genuinely fun for every driver in Nigeria — regardless of budget, experience, or where in the country they're racing from.

Our Story

HOW WE GOT HERE

2023

SIM Racing NG started as a small Discord server for Nigerian iRacing drivers to find each other.

2024

Grew past 1,000 members, launched the first community league, and hosted the first Lagos meetup.

2025

Added ACC and AC communities, ran the first 6-Hour Endurance Classic, and passed 2,000 members.

2026

SRN drivers swept the podium at the inaugural West Africa Sim Racing Cup — the community's biggest result yet.
